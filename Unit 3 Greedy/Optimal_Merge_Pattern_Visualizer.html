What is happening?

Optimal Merge Pattern: merging two files of sizes a and b costs a + b. To merge many files with minimum total cost, always pick the two smallest files, merge them, and put the result back. Each leaf's depth tells how many times that file is re-read, so small files go deep and large files stay near the root.

Total cost = sum of every internal node = Σ (size × depth of leaf). Greedy choice is provably optimal, like Huffman coding.
